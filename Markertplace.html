Platform Guidance & Anti-Spam Policy

Strict rules ensuring genuine GSM hardware and scam-free trading

1. Genuine Product Representation

All listings for GSM spare parts (IC chips, displays, ribbon flex cables) must clearly state whether they are OEM Original, High Copy, or Refurbished. Mislabeling grade is strictly prohibited.

2. Zero Tolerance Anti-Spam & Duplicate Postings

Vendors posting duplicate listings within 24 hours or submitting spam text in descriptions will face instant account suspension. Automated bots are actively monitoring marketplace posts.

3. Escrow & Secure Payment Advice

Never send direct wire transfers to unverified accounts. Always inspect repair equipment or devices upon arrival. Request video verification for high-value items.

4. Reporting Fraudulent Listings

If you discover a fake product, stolen IMEI phone, or spam listing, click the "Report Fraud / Spam" button on the item card or footer to alert site moderators instantly.
